Give Up Your Shoulds Day It's a day to stop stressing over something you keep telling yourself you should do, like exercising or getting organized. The laundry has been excused.

SUNDAY 11/1
National Cinnamon Day McCormick, the spice company, created it in 2019 to kick off what it calls Cinnamon Season. Somebody in marketing deserves a cinnamon roll.
SUNDAY 11/1
National Author's Day Nellie Verne Burt McPherson, president of a women's club in Bement, Illinois, thought it up in 1928 to thank her favorite author, Irving Bacheller. Fan mail, the original kind.
SUNDAY 11/1
National Calzone Day Calzone is Italian for "trousers," and it began in Naples as a street food for workers on the go. Pizza that came dressed for dinner.
SUNDAY 11/1
Prime Meridian Day In 1884, 25 nations met in Washington, D.C., to pick zero longitude: 22 voted for Greenwich, Haiti voted no, and France and Brazil abstained. Even the map needed a vote.
SUNDAY 11/1
World Vegan Day The word "vegan" was coined in November 1944, and Nov. 1 was picked because nobody knew the exact date. Somebody forgot to write it down.
SUNDAY 11/1
National Deep Fried Clams Day The clams usually deep fried are the soft-shelled kind, nicknamed "clam bellies" or "steamers." Nobody asks which belly they're eating.
MONDAY 11/2

Cookie Monster Day Cookie Monster's early role on Sesame Street was undefined and he didn't even have a name until the second season. Me glad he got a name.

National Deviled Egg Day Deviled eggs also go by eggs mimosa, stuffed eggs and dressed eggs. Whatever you call them, they vanish at potlucks.

Give Someone a Dollar Today Day You're supposed to give someone a dollar, whether a friend, a stranger or a charity. A dollar won't buy much, but it buys a smile.
TUESDAY 11/3
World Jellyfish Day Jellyfish are about 95 percent water, have no bones, and aren't even fish. So technically, wobbly water.
